Superbrain privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Superbrain is a game. This page says what it keeps about you, where it keeps it, and how to remove it. We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

  • Your progress: levels, ratings, streak, XP, badges, history of the cards you played, and your Brain Age scores.
  • Your settings: sound, music, reminder time, left-handed mode, and an optional birth year. The birth year is never sent anywhere. It only gives speed games a little extra time.
  • Mixes, quizzes and challenges you made.

You can wipe all of this at any time from the Brain tab (Developer tools in test builds) or by deleting the app.

What is sent to our server

Friends, leagues and weekly boards need a server. They run on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). When you use them, these are stored under your account:

  • A random account id from Firebase Authentication.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: the sign-in token and, if Apple or Google shares it, your email address (it can be Apple's private relay address).
  • Your public profile: the name you chose, your avatar, and your friend code.
  • Your weekly XP and league tier, so the boards can rank you. These scores are sent by your phone and are not verified.
  • Your friend connections.
  • Short activity lines (for example "reached Rank 6") and cheers you give. Only your friends can read your activity.

Anyone in your league can see your name, avatar and weekly XP. Your friends can also see your activity. Other players never see your email address.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. No advertising identifier.
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.
  • The handwriting in "Write it" games is read on your phone and is never stored or sent.

Superbrain Plus (subscription)

If you subscribe to Superbrain Plus, the purchase is handled by Apple through your Apple Account. Superbrain receives only whether your subscription is active, not your payment details, and stores that status on your device. You can cancel any time in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Superbrain schedules a local notification on your phone. Nothing is sent from a server.

Sharing links

When you share a challenge, mix or quiz, its content is packed into the link itself. Opening the link plays it on the other person's phone. Links go to the pungme.github.io page, which only forwards to the app and stores nothing.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open the Brain tab, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your Firebase account and removes your profile, friend code, name, weekly scores and friendships from our server. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app or reset it.

Children

Superbrain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. Friends and leagues need sign-in, so please do not use them under the age your country requires for an Apple or Google account.

Not medical advice

Superbrain is for fun and practice. "Brain Age" is a game score from 20 to 80, not a medical or psychological assessment, and the app does not diagnose or treat anything.

Who runs it

Superbrain is made by Worathiti Manosroi (pungme). Google Firebase processes the server data on our behalf under Google's terms.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the app version that needs it will say so in its notes. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/pungme/superbrain-web/issues.
